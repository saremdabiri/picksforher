## Is a Kindle a good gift for someone who loves to read?

Yes, for most readers. A Kindle holds thousands of books, weighs less than a paperback and reads like paper, without the glare of a phone or tablet. The Paperwhite Signature Edition is the best all-round pick, with an auto-adjusting light, wireless charging and up to 12 weeks per charge, according to Amazon. If she loves print books and refuses screens, skip the Kindle and give her the throw, the reading light or the book lamp instead.

## What's the best gift for a woman who reads in bed?

A warm reading light. Harvard Health reports that blue light at night suppresses melatonin, the sleep hormone, for about twice as long as green light of similar brightness ([Harvard Health](https://www.health.harvard.edu/staying-healthy/blue-light-has-a-dark-side)). So for paperbacks, pick a light with a warm amber setting, like the Glocusent's 3000K mode, and for Kindle readers, the Colorsoft's white-to-amber light. Add the Lamicall pillow stand so she can read without holding the device.

## Tips for choosing a gift for a book lover

- **Ask print or screen.** Some readers love their Kindle; others only read paper. Lights, throws and lamps work for both.
- **Check her Kindle first.** If she has a recent one, upgrade the reading spot instead of the device.
- **Add a book.** Tuck a new release from her favorite author, or an Amazon gift card for e-books, in with the gift.
- **Build a reading-night bundle.** A throw, a reading light and a pair of book socks make a complete gift under one bow.
