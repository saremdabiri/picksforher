## What is the best Christmas gift for a fiance (female)?

The best Christmas gift for a fiance is one that celebrates being engaged. A Swarovski Emily tennis bracelet adds sparkle without competing with her engagement ring, and the A Year of Us journal turns the months before the wedding into a keepsake. Engagements usually run a little over a year, according to [The Knot's research on engagement length](https://www.theknot.com/content/average-engagement-length), so this is likely your only Christmas as fiances. Make the gift feel like it belongs to this moment.

## What do you get your fiance for your first Christmas engaged?

For your first Christmas engaged, give one gift that looks back and one that looks ahead. The couple's journal captures how you feel now; a gift for your home together, like the Le Creuset Dutch oven or the Barefoot Dreams throw, is something you'll use for years. The UGG robe is a sweet bridge between the two: cozy now, and perfect for getting ready on the wedding day.

## What can I get my fiance if we're saving for the wedding?

If you're saving for the wedding, choose one small, personal gift and add a handwritten letter about the proposal. The couple's journal and the NEST candle cost far less than jewelry or a Dyson and still feel special.
