## When is it too late to order Christmas gifts for her?

You have more time than you think, but not unlimited. For the lower 48 states, the [USPS 2026 holiday shipping dates](https://about.usps.com/newsroom/national-releases/2026/0922-usps-recommends-2026-holiday-mailing-shipping-dates.htm) recommend sending by Dec. 17 for Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail, Dec. 18 for Priority Mail and Dec. 19 for Priority Mail Express. On Amazon, trust the delivery date shown under the price on the product page, and order a few days ahead of it to be safe.
