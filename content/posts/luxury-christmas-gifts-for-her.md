## How to choose

- **Think daily luxury.** Hair tools, fragrance and slippers get used every day.
- **Know her style.** Gold or silver, fresh or warm scents, structured or slouchy bags.
- **Make it a moment.** Beautiful wrapping and a handwritten note complete the gift.
