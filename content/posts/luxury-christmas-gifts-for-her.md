The best gifts for her are the ones she'd never buy for herself: the beautiful version of something she uses every day, or a small luxury that makes an ordinary evening feel special.

This is our Christmas edit, curated from houses and brands with genuine reputations for quality.

## How to choose

- **Think daily luxury.** Hair tools, fragrance and slippers get used every day.
- **Know her style.** Gold or silver, fresh or warm scents, structured or slouchy bags.
- **Make it a moment.** Beautiful wrapping and a handwritten note complete the gift.
