## How do you choose a luxury candle for her?

Think about the scents she already wears and the rooms she spends time in. If she loves **warm, cozy** perfumes, go for NEST Holiday or the Replica fragrance. If she likes **fresh and clean**, NEST Bamboo is the safe bet. If she already has a Capri Blue jar on the counter, a Volcano refill or diffuser is a gift she'll use for sure.

## Candle, diffuser or candle warmer?

A **candle** is the classic gift: glow and a full room of scent. A **reed diffuser** works all day with no flame, so it suits entryways, bathrooms and homes with pets. A **candle warmer lamp** makes every candle last longer and is a nice add-on to either.

## Tips for buying luxury candle gifts

- **Buy a holiday scent in November.** Seasonal favorites like NEST Holiday sell out close to Christmas.
- **Go bigger for a statement.** A 3-wick candle feels like a real gift, not a stocking filler.
- **Match the room.** Fresh scents suit bathrooms; warm scents suit living rooms.
- **Pair it.** Candle plus matching diffuser makes a full set.
