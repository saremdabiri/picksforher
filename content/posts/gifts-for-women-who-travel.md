## What do you buy a woman who travels a lot?

Buy something she'll use on every trip, not a souvenir-style trinket. Frequent travelers already have a passport holder; what they want is less friction: quieter flights, lighter bags, a hair dryer that works abroad, and a way to see where the suitcase went. Everything on this page fits in or on a carry-on, so it never becomes one more thing to leave at home.

## What should she keep in her carry-on?

Anything with a lithium battery that isn't installed in a device, like power banks and spare batteries, belongs in her carry-on. The FAA says spare lithium batteries and power banks "must be carried with the passenger in carry-on baggage and remain accessible" ([FAA](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/lithium-batteries-baggage)). That's another reason a roomy tote like the Michael Kors Voyager makes a good travel gift: it holds the chargers, earbuds and Kindle in one place.

## Which travel gift is the best luxury pick?

The Dyson Supersonic Travel is the one she's least likely to buy herself. Dyson says it's 32% smaller and 25% lighter than its full-size Supersonic and adapts to any voltage, so it works in Paris as well as at home. For a bigger gift, pair it with the Samsonite carry-on. More high-end ideas are in our [luxury Christmas gifts for her](/luxury-christmas-gifts-for-her/) guide.

## Tips for choosing a travel gift

- **Check her phone.** AirPods Pro 3 and AirTags work best with an iPhone.
- **Think about her trips.** Work travelers love the tote and earbuds; vacationers love the Kindle and dryer.
- **Size the luggage.** Airline carry-on limits vary, so check her usual airline before buying a suitcase.
- **Add a small extra.** The jewelry box or AirTags tuck into a bigger gift as a bonus.
