## How to choose an anniversary gift for your wife

- **Go personal.** Jewelry and fragrance feel intimate in a way gadgets don't.
- **Match the milestone.** A first anniversary can be sweet and simple; a 10th or 25th deserves something she'll keep for years.
- **Upgrade her everyday.** The hair tool, the watch, the throw on the sofa: the luxury version of what she already loves.
- **Add a handwritten note.** It's the part she'll remember most.
