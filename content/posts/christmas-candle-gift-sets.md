## How to build your own Christmas candle gift set

- **Pick one scent family.** Spicy and woody (NEST Holiday) or fresh and sweet (Capri Blue Volcano). Matching scents look intentional.
- **Mix sizes.** One big candle plus a votive or diffuser feels like a set, not a single jar.
- **Add a flame-free option.** A reed diffuser keeps the scent going in rooms where she won't light a candle.
- **Wrap it simply.** A box, tissue and a ribbon are enough; these jars are the decoration.
