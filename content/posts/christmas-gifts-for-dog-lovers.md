## What is a good Christmas gift for a dog lover?

The best gift solves a small daily problem she has with her dog or gives her a treat she wouldn't buy herself. Think about her routine: does she worry about the dog while she's at work (a camera), walk or hike every day (a great harness), travel with the dog (a travel bag) or fight muddy paws and accidents (a spot cleaner)? Match the gift to that, and it gets used every single day.

## Is a dog camera a good gift?

Yes, if she's away from home for part of the day. A camera lets her see, talk to and reward her dog from her phone. Pick one that doesn't require a paid plan so the gift doesn't come with a bill: Fur and Nest's guide to the best [dog camera with no subscription](https://thefurandnest.com/dog-camera-no-subscription/) compares six of them.
