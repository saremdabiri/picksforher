Jewelry is the gift that gets worn on the best days of her life, and on all the ordinary ones in between. The trick is choosing pieces with real quality that suit how she actually dresses.

This edit focuses on four gifts that feel special, last beautifully and go with almost everything.

<!-- list -->

## What to look for

- **Metal.** Solid 14K gold or sterling silver wears best and won't tarnish easily.
- **Her style.** Delicate and everyday, or statement and evening.
- **Certification and care.** For diamonds, look for grading details; keep the original box and papers.
- **Presentation.** A beautiful box and a handwritten note make the moment.
