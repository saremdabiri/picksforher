## How to choose a pearl gift

Look at what she already wears. If her jewelry is dainty, choose a pendant or studs; if she dresses up often, a full strand or drop earrings will get worn. Match the metal she wears most (white or yellow gold), and pick a pearl size that suits her: 7-8mm is classic and easy to wear.

Grade matters more than size. Higher grades (AAAA) mean rounder pearls with more shine. For a milestone, buy one better piece rather than several small ones, then add matching earrings next year.
