## What is a romantic gift for a wife?

A romantic gift is personal, not just pricey. It says "I know you" or "I remember us": your photos in a frame she sees every day, a charm for the trip you took together, or the perfume she wore on your first date. Jewelry is romantic when it marks a moment, like an anniversary, so add a handwritten card that says why. A practical gadget, however expensive, rarely feels romantic.

## Are lab-grown diamond earrings a good romantic gift?

Yes, if she likes diamonds. Lab-grown diamonds are real diamonds grown in a lab instead of mined, so you can usually give a larger or better stone for the money. The FTC says sellers must describe lab-created stones accurately and make clear they aren't mined ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2019/04/warning-letters-re-mined-diamond-sellers-describe-products-accurately)), so check that the listing says "lab grown" or "lab created" up front.

## How do you make a gift more romantic?

Add the story. Load the photo frame with pictures from your years together, pick a bracelet charm with your wedding date, or recreate a first-date detail like her perfume or the restaurant.
