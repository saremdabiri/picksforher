## What do friends actually want for Christmas?

Most people want practical, everyday gifts. According to the [National Retail Federation's 2026 holiday data](https://nrf.com/research-insights/holiday-data-and-trends/winter-holidays), 47% of shoppers want gift cards, 44% want clothing and 24% want personal care or beauty items. For friends, clothing is risky because of sizes, so this list sticks to beauty, candles and small self-care upgrades she'll reach for all winter.
