## How do you choose a coffee gift for her?

Start with **how she drinks it**. If she orders lattes and cappuccinos, an espresso machine is the big, memorable gift. If she wants speed, a Nespresso is the smarter pick. If she loves a slow morning with pour-over or tea, the Fellow kettle is the design-lover's choice.

Then think about **effort**. The Breville machines reward a little practice; the De'Longhi does everything with one tap.

## Tips for buying coffee gifts

- **Add the beans.** Tuck a bag of her favorite local roast in with the machine.
- **Check the counter.** Espresso machines need space under the cabinets.
- **Pair big and small.** A machine plus the milk frother or a set of pretty mugs makes a complete gift.
