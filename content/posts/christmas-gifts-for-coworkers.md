## What is an appropriate Christmas gift for a coworker?

An appropriate Christmas gift for a coworker is simple, moderately priced and not personal. The Emily Post Institute's [office gift-giving pointers](https://emilypost.com/advice/five-holiday-gift-giving-pointers-for-the-office) recommend keeping work gifts simple, choosing food over alcohol for corporate gifts, and avoiding personal items like perfume, cologne or pajamas. Hand cream, lip care, a tumbler or a desk plant fits all of those rules and still feels like a treat.

## How do I buy Christmas gifts for a whole office?

Pick one gift you can buy in identical multiples so nobody compares. Boxed sets like the Burt's Bees Tips and Toes Kit or the Hand Repair set arrive gift-ready, and lip masks or eye patches slip into small bags with a handwritten tag. If your team has a spending limit or a Secret Santa, follow it; if not, keep everyone's gift the same to avoid awkwardness.

## Should I get my boss a Christmas gift?

You don't have to. Etiquette guidance generally says gifts should flow down, not up, and the Emily Post Institute suggests a group gift from the team if you want to thank a manager, with contributions kept optional. Pool the money for one nicer gift, such as the Capri Blue Volcano candle or a YETI tumbler, and have everyone sign one card.
