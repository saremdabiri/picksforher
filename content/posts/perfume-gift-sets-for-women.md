## How to choose a perfume gift set

- **Look at her current bottle.** Floral, sweet or woody: stay close to what she already wears.
- **Sets beat bottles for gifting.** The lotion and mini make it feel more generous, and the box is already gift-ready.
- **Not sure? Go discovery.** Replica minis let her find a favorite, and they make elegant stocking stuffers.
- **Season matters.** By the Fireplace and Good Girl are perfect for winter; Bright Crystal, Idôle and Flowerbomb work all year.
