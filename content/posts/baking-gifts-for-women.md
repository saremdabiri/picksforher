## What is the best baking gift for a woman who has everything?

If she already owns a stand mixer, go deeper into what she bakes. Bread bakers want a proofing basket and starter kit; pie bakers love an heirloom ceramic dish like Emile Henry; cookie bakers wear out sheet pans and want a second one. A cookbook from a baker she admires is a personal touch that costs little and gets used.

## Why do serious bakers weigh their flour?

Scooping flour with a cup packs it down, so the amount changes every time. King Arthur Baking says a cup of all-purpose flour should weigh 120 grams, but a densely packed cup can hold up to 160 grams ([King Arthur Baking](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2023/10/13/measure-flour)). That difference is enough to turn soft cookies dry, which is why a digital scale is the most useful small gift for any baker.
