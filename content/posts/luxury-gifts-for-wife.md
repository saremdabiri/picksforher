## How to choose a luxury gift for your wife

- **Choose something she'll use often.** Jewelry she wears daily, a hair tool she uses every morning, a scent she reaches for.
- **Upgrade, don't duplicate.** The flagship version of something she already loves lands better than a novelty.
- **Think about the occasion.** Jewelry suits an anniversary or a big birthday; fragrance, a throw or silk make lovely second gifts at Christmas.
- **Check the details.** Her metal preference (yellow or white gold), her favorite scent family, and her hair type for styling tools.
