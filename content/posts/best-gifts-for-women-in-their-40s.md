## How to choose a gift for a woman in her 40s

- **Choose proven classics.** Cult products like Advanced Night Repair have earned their reputation over years.
- **Buy from the brand or Amazon.** For skincare, fragrance and jewelry, the seller matters as much as the product.
- **Upgrade a ritual.** Her morning styling, evening skincare or Sunday reading are perfect places for a little luxury.
- **Keep it personal.** A necklace or a signature scent says more than a gadget.
