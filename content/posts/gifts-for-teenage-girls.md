## What do teenage girls want for Christmas?

Most teenage girls want what their friends are wearing and posting: viral beauty from brands like Sol de Janeiro and LANEIGE, UGGs, and good headphones. Teens also do a lot of their shopping on Amazon: Piper Sandler's fall 2025 Taking Stock With Teens survey found Amazon is teens' top shopping website by a wide margin ([Piper Sandler](https://www.pipersandler.com/news/piper-sandler-completes-50th-semi-annual-teen-survey)). Picking a listing sold by Amazon or the brand also helps you avoid fakes in beauty.

## What are good Christmas gifts for teenage girls by age?

At 12 to 14, keep it fun and gentle: the LANEIGE lip mask, a Sol de Janeiro mist, the LEGO Botanicals bouquet or UGG Tazz II. At 14 to 16, Beats Solo 4 headphones or a Shark FlexStyle become the big gift. At 16 to 18, practical upgrades land: AirPods 5, an Apple Watch SE 3 for iPhone users, or the Glow Recipe serum for her routine.

## How to choose a gift for a teenage girl

- **Look at what she saves and shares.** Viral beauty (Sol de Janeiro, LANEIGE) and UGGs are on most wish lists this year.
- **Go genuine.** Beauty and hair tools sold by Amazon or the brand avoid fakes.
- **Mix one big gift with small ones.** A FlexStyle or headphones, plus a lip mask or scrunchies for the stocking.
