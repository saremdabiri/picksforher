A great hair tool is a daily luxury. She'll use it more than almost anything else you give her, which is exactly why it's worth choosing well.

Dyson and Shark lead this category, but they're built for slightly different routines. Here's how to choose.

<!-- list -->

## What to look for

- **Dryer or styler?** Pure drying power vs. curls and blowouts from one tool.
- **Hair type.** Fine, thick or curly hair all benefit from different attachments.
- **Weight and noise.** Matters if she styles daily.
- **What's included.** Kits vary; check attachments and storage.
