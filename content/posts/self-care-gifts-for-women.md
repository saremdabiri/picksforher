## How do you choose a self care gift for her?

Think about how she already unwinds. A skincare lover will love a prestige moisturizer or a new beauty tool, a woman who's always on the go will use a pocket massager, and a homebody will live in a plush robe or a soft throw. The best self care gifts don't add a chore; they make something she already does feel more special.

## Build a self care gift basket

Pairing two or three picks makes a gift feel personal. Try the **UGG Aarti robe + Slip pillowcase** for a cozy night-in set, **TATCHA Water Cream + TheraFace wand** for a skincare lover, or the **TheraGun Mini + Barefoot Dreams throw** for the woman who never sits down.

## Tips for buying self care gifts

- **Stick to brand-sold listings** for skincare so you get the real thing.
- **Skip anything that sounds like a hint.** Frame it as a treat, not a fix.
- **Add a note** that gives her the time to use it: "Sunday morning is yours."
