## How to choose a luxury gift for mom

- **Upgrade her favorites.** If she already uses Estée Lauder or loves a certain scent, the larger or prestige version is a safe win.
- **Luxury beauty is the easiest splurge.** Skincare and perfume feel indulgent but get used every day.
- **Choose pieces that last.** Swarovski jewelry, Le Creuset cookware and silk bedding are built for years, not one season.
- **Buy from the brand.** For perfume, skincare and jewelry, buy from Amazon.com or the brand's own store to avoid counterfeits. Every pick here qualifies.
