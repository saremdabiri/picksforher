## What's the best Christmas gift for mom from a daughter?

Daughters usually know Mom's routines best, so upgrade one of them. If she lives in sweaters, the Barefoot Dreams throw is the classic choice. If she has a skincare ritual, Estée Lauder Advanced Night Repair is a luxe refill she'll actually finish. Want it to feel personal? Pair the gift with a handwritten card about a favorite memory together. That costs nothing and is often what she keeps longest.

## What should a son get his mom for Christmas?

Sons tend to do best with gifts that don't depend on her clothing size or taste in decor. A Kindle Colorsoft for a reader, a KitchenAid stand mixer for a baker, or a TheraGun Mini for a mom who's on her feet all day are simple, useful and easy to buy online. Add a note that says what the gift is for, like "for your Sunday mornings."

## How to choose a Christmas gift for mom

- **Think daily comfort.** Throws, slippers and coffee rituals get used every day of winter.
- **Upgrade her kitchen or vanity.** Quality cookware or a premium hair tool replaces something she already uses.
- **Go timeless.** Jewelry and a signature scent become keepsakes.
