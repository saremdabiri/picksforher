**Looking for the best luxury perfume for women to give this Christmas?** A designer fragrance is one of the most personal gifts you can give. These are eight luxury perfumes women love most, chosen for their reputation, thousands of reviews and the bottles that look stunning on a dresser: **Lancôme La Vie Est Belle**, **Armani My Way** and **Carolina Herrera Good Girl** lead the list.

Every bottle on this page was a full-size listing sold by the brand on Amazon when we checked, which matters for perfume because counterfeits are common from unknown sellers. We haven't worn every scent here.

## A quick guide to scent families

- **Warm and sweet (gourmand):** vanilla, praline and tonka. La Vie Est Belle, Good Girl.
- **Rich floral:** jasmine, rose and orchid. Flowerbomb, Born in Roma.
- **Fresh floral:** citrus, rose and orange blossom. My Way, Idôle, Acqua di Gioia Intense.
- **Soft and clean:** musk, soap and soft florals. Maison Margiela Replica Bubble Bath.

## Full bottle or gift set?

A full bottle of a scent she already wears is the safest luxury gift.
