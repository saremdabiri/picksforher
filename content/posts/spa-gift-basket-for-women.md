## How do you build a spa gift basket for her?

Start with a **base**: a bathtub tray or a folded robe looks better than a wicker basket and she'll keep using it. Add **one product for each step** of a spa night: wash, soak, body cream and face. Finish with **one bathroom upgrade**, like the Aesop hand wash, that she'll see every day.

## Pre-made bath gift set or build your own?

A **ready-made bath gift set** like the OSEA set is quick and looks polished. **Building your own** spa basket lets you pick her favorite scents and include a big-ticket item like the robe. Pick three to five products; more than that starts to look like clutter.

## Tips for buying spa gifts

- **Match her scent.** Sweet (Sol de Janeiro), nutty (L'Occitane), woody (Aesop) or subtle (OSEA).
- **Mind sensitive skin.** If she's sensitive, lean on the OSEA set and skip strong fragrance.
- **Wrap it in cellophane.** On a bath tray, the whole basket looks like it came from a spa boutique.
