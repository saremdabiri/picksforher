## How to choose the right UGG for her

- **Boot or slipper?** If she'll wear them outside every day, pick a Classic boot. If she lives in slippers at home, the Tasman II or Coquette is the safer choice.
- **Platform or flat?** The Ultra Mini Platform and Tazz II are the trend picks; the Classic Short II and Classic Mini II are timeless.
- **Color.** Chestnut is the classic UGG shade; black and sand are the easiest to wear with everything.
- **Size.** Sheepskin stretches a little, so a snug fit at first is normal. Check her current shoe size before ordering.
- **Make it a set.** A robe plus slippers turns one gift into a big one.
