## How to choose a gift for a woman in her 60s

- **Choose the classics she trusts.** Advanced Night Repair, Génifique and SK-II are long-loved luxury beauty icons.
- **Buy from the brand or Amazon.** For skincare and fragrance, the seller matters as much as the product.
- **Make tech easy.** An iPad mini or Kindle is simple to set up, and you can load photos or books before you wrap it.
- **Add comfort.** A plush throw turns her favorite chair into the best seat in the house.
