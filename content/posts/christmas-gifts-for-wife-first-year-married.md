## What do you get your wife for your first Christmas married?

Get her one thing that marks the year and one thing for your life together. A keepsake like the Swarovski Una Angelic necklace says "this was our first Christmas," while a matching robe, a Barefoot Dreams throw or a Dutch oven for your first home turns the gift into something you share. Finish with a handwritten note about a favorite moment from your wedding or first months married; it's the part she'll keep longest.

## What is a sentimental first Christmas gift for a new wife?

Paper is the [traditional first-anniversary gift](https://www.theknot.com/content/paper-anniversary-gifts), so a love letter, a printed wedding photo or a favorite book slipped into the box is a sentimental nod to your first year. Pair it with a lasting item, such as the necklace or a candle you light every Christmas Eve, so the memory has something she can hold.

## How much should you spend on your wife's first Christmas gift?

There's no rule. Spend what fits your budget as newlyweds, and put the thought into the choice instead. A smaller keepsake plus a note often lands better than a splurge she didn't want. If you're saving for a home or a honeymoon, the candle and a love letter are a lovely first-year combination.
