## How do you choose a jewelry gift for your wife?

Start with **what she already wears**. Look at her jewelry box: silver or gold, dainty or bold, hoops or studs. Match the metal first, then the style. If she wears a watch every day, a **tennis bracelet** is a sure thing. If she dresses up often, a **statement necklace** or **pearls** will get worn more.

## Tennis bracelet, pearls or a pendant?

A **tennis bracelet** is the classic anniversary-level gift. **Pearls** are timeless and suit a more traditional style. A **pendant** is the easiest everyday piece, and a great add-on to a bigger gift. For a Christmas morning set, pair a bracelet with the matching Millenia hoops.

## Tips for buying jewelry gifts

- **Buy from the brand or Amazon.com.** Jewelry is a common counterfeit category, so check who sells the listing.
- **Check the bracelet length.** Most tennis bracelets come in sizes; measure a bracelet she already owns.
- **Order early.** Popular Swarovski pieces sell out in December.
