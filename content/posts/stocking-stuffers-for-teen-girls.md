## How to choose stocking stuffers for a teen girl

Start with one lip product; it's the stocking stuffer almost every teen wants. Then add one item from a different category, a scent, a skincare product or a hair accessory, so the stocking doesn't feel repetitive. For beauty, buy from listings sold by Amazon.com or the brand to avoid fakes, and check the scent or shade she likes if you can.

Younger teens do best with simple, fun products like lip balms and scrunchies. Older teens, especially 16 to 19, appreciate a small luxury pick like Tatcha or a Sol de Janeiro mist.
