## How do you choose a designer handbag gift?

Look at **the bag she carries now**. If she lives out of a big tote, choose the kate spade Bleecker or a Michael Kors tote. If she's always reaching for a small bag, the Jet Set crossbody is the safer bet. Stick to **neutral colors** (black, brown, cream) unless you know her style, and keep the gift receipt.
