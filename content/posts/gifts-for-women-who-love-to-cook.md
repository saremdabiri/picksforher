## How to choose a kitchen gift for a woman who loves to cook

Look at what she cooks most, then upgrade that one tool. A baker gets the most from a stand mixer, a soup-and-stew cook from a Dutch oven, and someone who makes dinner every night from a great knife or better pans. Check her kitchen first: if she already owns a KitchenAid, a Staub in a new color is a better surprise than a second mixer.

## Is a stand mixer or a Dutch oven the better gift?

Both are classic "she'll have it forever" gifts. Pick the stand mixer if she bakes, and the Dutch oven if she cooks savory meals. If you have the budget, the two together make a gift set she'll use all winter.

## Tips for giving a cooking gift

- **Match her colors.** KitchenAid, Le Creuset and Staub come in many finishes; peek at her counter.
- **Mind the weight.** Cast iron Dutch ovens are heavy; pick a smaller size if lifting is hard.
- **Add the care note.** Japanese knives need hand washing.
- **Pair it with a recipe.** A handwritten favorite recipe in the card makes it personal.
