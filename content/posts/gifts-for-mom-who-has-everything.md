## What do you buy a mom who says she wants nothing?

Buy her an upgrade or a memory, not more stuff. Moms who "have everything" usually have plenty of things but rarely treat themselves to the nicer version of what they use every day, like a silk pillowcase or new slippers. The other gift that always lands is family photos. A Wi-Fi frame that everyone can add to turns one gift into a year of new pictures, which is why it's our top pick.

## What do moms actually want for Christmas?

Most moms want the same things most shoppers want: the [National Retail Federation's 2026 holiday data](https://nrf.com/research-insights/holiday-data-and-trends/winter-holidays) puts gift cards first at 47%, then clothing at 44%, books and media at 25% and personal care or beauty at 24%. So a cozy wearable like UGG slippers, a Kindle for books, or a pampering pick like a silk pillowcase covers what she's most likely to want, and feels more personal than a gift card.

## Tips for choosing a gift for mom who has everything

- **Upgrade, don't add.** Replace something she uses daily (slippers, pillowcase, blanket) with a nicer version.
- **Make it shared.** Ask siblings to load photos onto the Aura frame before you wrap it.
- **Think about her evenings.** Throws, heated wraps and a Kindle get used every night.
- **Check sizes.** For slippers, look at her current pair before ordering.
