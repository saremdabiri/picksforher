## What is a good Christmas gift for a cat lover?

The best gift solves a small daily thing in her life with the cat or spoils them both. Think about her routine: is the cat always on the laptop or the radiator (a heated bed), glued to the window (a perch), home alone all day (a camera) or drinking from the faucet (a fountain)? Match the gift to that and it gets used every single day, which is the point.

## Is a cat camera a good gift?

Yes, if she's away from home for part of the day. A camera lets her see and talk to her cat from her phone. Pick one that doesn't need a paid plan so the gift doesn't come with a monthly bill. The Tapo C210 on this list records to a memory card for free; Fur and Nest compares more in its guide to the best [dog camera with no subscription](https://thefurandnest.com/dog-camera-no-subscription/) (they work just as well for cats).
