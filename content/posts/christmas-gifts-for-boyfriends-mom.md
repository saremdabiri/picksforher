## How to choose a Christmas gift for your boyfriend's mom

Ask your boyfriend two things: what does she do to relax, and what does her kitchen or living room look like? If she loves a slow evening, go cozy (the throw, a candle). If she hosts, go home (Aesop, NEST). If she cooks, go kitchen (Le Creuset, Nespresso). Luxury body care from Kiehl's or L'Occitane works for almost every mom, and it's far safer than perfume, clothing or jewelry when you're still getting to know her.

Keep it classy and add a handwritten card thanking her for having you. If you've only been dating a few months, one beautiful small gift is better than a big one; save the Le Creuset for a joint gift with your boyfriend.

If you're spending the holidays with his family, yes. A gift for each parent, or one shared gift for the home, is a thoughtful gesture. For his dad, see [Christmas gifts for boyfriend's dad](https://picksforhim.com/christmas-gifts-for-boyfriends-dad/) on our sister site Picks for Him.
