## What do sisters actually want for Christmas?

Sisters usually want the things the rest of the country wants: gift cards, clothing and beauty. The NRF's 2026 holiday figures put gift cards first at 47%, clothing at 44%, books and media at 25% and personal care or beauty at 24%. That's why this list leans on cozy wear, beauty and home comfort. If you're stuck between two picks, choose the one she'd use every day, like slippers or a throw, over a one-time novelty.

## What's a good Christmas gift for a sister who has everything?

For a sister who has everything, give an upgrade or an activity instead of more stuff. A LEGO Botanicals Mini Orchid is a calm evening build that becomes decor, a Slip silk pillowcase upgrades something she uses every night, and a candle warmer lamp makes the candles she already owns last longer. These are the kind of small luxuries people skip buying for themselves, which is what makes them feel like a real gift.

## What should a brother get his sister?

A brother should pick a gift that doesn't depend on her exact size or taste. The Fiosnow blanket hoodie is oversized and gets a laugh, the GODONLIF candle warmer lamp looks good anywhere, and the Barefoot Dreams throw is a safe bet for nearly everyone. If you have other siblings, pool your money for the Dyson Supersonic Travel hair dryer and give it from all of you.
