## What makes a good white elephant gift for women?

A good white elephant gift for women is something most people would use but wouldn't buy for themselves, wrapped so it's a surprise. Cozy and home gifts work best because they suit almost anyone: candles, warm wraps, blankets and nice kitchen tools. Avoid sizes, strong scents or anything too personal, since you don't know who will end up with it.

A quick test: if three different women in your group would all be happy to take it home, it's a good pick.

## Funny or useful: which white elephant gift for women wins?

Useful gifts usually win the stealing round; funny gifts win the laugh. In most groups the most-stolen gifts are practical treats like a candle warmer lamp or a heated wrap, while a gag gift like The Screaming Goat gets one big laugh and then stays put. If you can't decide, pick something useful with a fun twist, like the blanket hoodie.

Always follow the group's rules. Some exchanges are "funny only" and some are "useful only."

## How do you pick a white elephant gift for a mixed group?

For a group of men and women, choose a gift that doesn't depend on gender: the candle warmer lamp, the electric grinder set, the heated neck wrap or The Screaming Goat all work for anyone. Skip beauty and skincare items unless the group is mostly women.

Stay inside the spending limit, wrap it well (a great wrap makes people pick it first), and if it needs batteries, include them.

## White elephant rules in 30 seconds

- Everyone brings one wrapped gift, usually within a set spending limit.
- Players draw numbers. The first person opens a gift.
- Each next person either opens a new gift or **steals** one already opened.
- Most groups cap steals (often 2 or 3 per gift) so the game ends.
