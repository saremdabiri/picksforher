## How do you choose a tennis bracelet gift?

Match the **metal** she already wears first: silver tone for the Swarovski pieces, gold for Michael Kors. Then choose the **width**. A slim tennis bracelet like the Emily works every day; a wider one like the Matrix is for evenings out. If she wears a watch, a slim bracelet stacks better.

## Tennis bracelet or pearl necklace?

A **tennis bracelet** is the modern classic, the anniversary-level piece that goes with everything. A **pearl necklace** suits a more traditional, polished style and works for a mom or grandmother too. For a Christmas morning set, pair a bracelet with the matching Una Angelic studs.

## Tips for buying jewelry gifts

- **Buy from the brand or Amazon.com.** Check who sells the listing before you buy.
- **Check the size.** Measure a bracelet she owns.
- **Order early.** Popular Swarovski pieces sell out in December.
