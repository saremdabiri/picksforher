## What do you get a new mom?

Get her something that is only for her. According to the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/breastfeeding/how-much-and-how-often.html), most exclusively breastfed babies feed every 2 to 4 hours, day and night, so comfort gifts go a long way: a plush UGG Aarti robe, a Slip silk pillowcase, a Kindle Paperwhite she can hold in one hand or a YETI tumbler that keeps her coffee hot. Add an Aura frame so family photos of the baby land right on her nightstand.

## What is a good push gift?

A push gift (or push present) is a gift for the new mom to mark the birth. Jewelry is the classic choice because she can wear it for years: simple lab-grown diamond studs are elegant, everyday and safe around a baby, unlike long necklaces or dangling earrings. If jewelry isn't her style, an Aura frame filled with the first photos is just as meaningful.

## How to choose a gift for a new mom

- **Make it about her.** The baby will get plenty of gifts; she is the one who needs a treat.
- **Think one-handed.** Anything she can use while holding a baby wins: a Kindle, a tumbler with a lid, a robe with no fiddly buttons.
- **Keep the memories.** A digital frame or a photo gift lasts long after the newborn weeks.
- **Add help.** A note offering a night shift, a meal or a nap is a gift too.
