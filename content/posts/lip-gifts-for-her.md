## How to choose a lip gift for her

Think about how she wears her lips. If she loves shine, pick a lip oil (Dior or Summer Fridays) or the LANEIGE Glowy Balm. If she cares more about skincare, an overnight mask like LANEIGE or Tatcha is the pick. For a little color, Clinique Black Honey and Lancôme Butterglow are sheer enough to suit almost anyone.

For a bigger gift, pair two: an overnight mask for night and a glossy oil or tinted balm for day. Buy beauty from listings sold by Amazon.com or the brand, like every pick here.
