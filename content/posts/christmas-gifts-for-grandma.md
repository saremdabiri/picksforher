## What do grandmas really want for Christmas?

Most grandmas want time with family, photos of the grandkids and small comforts that make everyday life easier. That's why a digital frame tops this list: the family keeps adding photos all year. According to the [National Retail Federation's 2026 holiday data](https://nrf.com/research-insights/holiday-data-and-trends/winter-holidays), clothing and personal care are among the most-wanted gifts, so cozy picks like a throw or slippers are safe bets too.

## What is a good Christmas gift for grandma from grandkids?

The best gift from grandkids is one with the grandkids in it. Load the Aura frame with photos before wrapping, build the LEGO Botanicals bouquet together on Christmas afternoon, or tuck a handwritten card and a recent school photo in with a small gift like the Glocusent reading light. Younger kids can split a bigger gift with parents and sign the card.

## What gifts are easy for grandma to use?

Look for big buttons, no complicated setup and nothing she has to charge every day. The Aura frame only needs Wi-Fi once, the heated wrap has one simple control, and the Kindle lets her enlarge the text. Set up anything with an app before Christmas Day so it's ready the moment she opens it.
