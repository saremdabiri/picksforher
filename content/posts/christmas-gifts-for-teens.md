## How do you choose a Christmas gift for a teen?

Think about **how she spends her day**. The teen who's always getting ready loves a Dyson; the one who's always on the go wants a smartwatch or headphones; the reader wants a Kindle. If you're not sure, **beauty from a brand she follows** is the safe bet, and a big gift plus one beauty stocking stuffer always lands.

## Tips for buying gifts for teens

- **Ask a friend or sibling.** Teens are specific about brands and colors.
- **Check her phone.** Apple Watch needs an iPhone.
- **Buy boots early.** Popular UGG sizes sell out in December.
- **Gift receipts help.** Especially for shoes and clothing.
