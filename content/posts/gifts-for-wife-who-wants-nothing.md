## What does a wife really mean when she says she wants nothing?

Usually she means she doesn't want clutter, or she doesn't want you to spend on something she'd never use. So skip decor and gadgets that need a shelf. Pick things that get used every day (a silk pillowcase, a robe, a throw), things that get used up (a candle, a lip mask), or things that hold memories (a photo frame). Those feel thoughtful without adding stuff.

## Why are consumable gifts a smart choice for her?

A consumable gift is one that gets used up, like a candle, skincare or a favorite treat. It delivers a little luxury for weeks and then disappears, so it never becomes clutter. Consumables are also easy to pair: wrap a NEST candle with a handwritten card about a date night you've planned, and the gift becomes time together, which is often what "nothing" really means.
